## Visual system

- Canvas: opaque near-black.
- Dark surface: opaque graphite with a precise neutral rule.
- Decision surface: opaque warm ivory with ink-black content.
- Accent: solid ultraviolet for the primary action and selected state only.
- Live signal: green; cyan and gold are secondary semantic signals only.
- Display typography: Anton, uppercase and condensed, paired with Inter for UI and reading.
- Geometry: 4–16px radii; pills are reserved for compact status or segmented controls.
- No gradients, backdrop blur, frosted surfaces, glowing logo containers, or decorative glass.

## Interaction contract

- Only links, buttons and explicitly interactive roles receive hover, press, cursor or sound feedback.
- Static cards, status chips and metadata blocks remain visually still.
- Every card that promises navigation owns a complete semantic hit area.
- Motion communicates entry, selection and state change; reduced-motion removes transforms and loops.

## Product contract

- The first desktop viewport pairs the existing promise and CTAs with a real live Radar surface.
- The decision journey is action-based: Scopri, Confronta, Salva. No points or fabricated achievements.
- Radar nodes/table, catalog filters, compare actions, shortlist actions, search, navigation and account CTAs remain functional.
- Mobile preserves reachability and a single-column reading order.
