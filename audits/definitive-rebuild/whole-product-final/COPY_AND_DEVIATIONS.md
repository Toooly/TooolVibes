# Copy diff and intentional deviations

## Copy changes

Broad editorial copy and critical legal/product content were preserved.
Changes are limited to:

- canonical navigation vocabulary: Home, Catalogo, Radar, Confronto, Salvati;
- concise search, filter and disclosure labels;
- factual unavailable language for Product Hunt data;
- accessibility labels and state announcements;
- score labels that distinguish editorial `x/10` from battle `x/100`.

No tool claim, metric, score, review, collection slot or Product Hunt item was
invented. Blink remains `8.4` wherever an editorial score is shown.

## Intentional concept deviations

- Image-generated records, numbers and account capabilities were treated as
  visual placeholders and never copied into runtime data.
- The generated concepts used representative density; production surfaces keep
  complete access through real filtering and progressive disclosure.
- The 1280px shell switch follows measured product density rather than a
  conventional 1024px desktop breakpoint.
- Radar favours a truthful paused/unavailable state over a visually richer fake
  feed.
- Account keeps the existing auth provider and roles; the contextual split is
  presentational only.
- The existing warm-ivory collection and editorial surfaces remain because they
  encode the Decision Desk layer of the product system.
